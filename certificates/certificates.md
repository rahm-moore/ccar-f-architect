# Certificate Registry

Master log of every certificate earned across all training programs. The certificate
files themselves live in Google Drive — this table just points to them, so the repo
stays lightweight and doesn't store binary/PDF credentials.

**How to add an entry:**
1. Upload the certificate PDF/image to Google Drive (suggested folder: `Training Certificates/<provider>/`).
2. Get a shareable link (Share → "Anyone with the link" or restricted, your call).
3. Add a row below.
